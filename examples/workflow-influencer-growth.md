# Multi-Agent Workflow: Influencer Growth & Monetization

> A step-by-step guide to using the Influencer Growth Suite to audit your content, engineer viral posts, build your community, and close your first (or next) brand deal.

## The Scenario

You're a lifestyle creator with 8,200 Instagram followers and a TikTok with 12,400 followers. You've been posting consistently for 8 months but growth has stalled. Engagement feels random. You haven't landed a paid brand deal yet. You know you have something — you just need a system to unlock it.

This workflow takes you from stalled to scaling in 5 coordinated phases.

## Agent Team

| Agent | Role in this workflow |
|-------|---------------------|
| Influencer Growth Coach | Full account audit, goal setting, 90-day roadmap |
| Content Performance Analyst | Decode what's working and why — and what's killing reach |
| Viral Content Architect | Engineer the next 30 days of content with proven formulas |
| Audience Intelligence Agent | Map superfans, build community strategy, define niche authority |
| Brand Deal Strategist | Create media kit, set rates, launch brand outreach campaign |

## The Workflow

### Phase 1: Account Audit & Goal Setting

**Step 1 — Activate Influencer Growth Coach**

```
Activate Influencer Growth Coach.

Here is my account overview:
- Platforms: Instagram (@handle) and TikTok (@handle)
- Instagram: 8,200 followers, ~3.1% average engagement rate, posting 4x/week
- TikTok: 12,400 followers, ~6.8% average engagement rate, posting 3x/week
- Niche: Lifestyle — sustainable living, minimalism, and intentional home design
- Content formats I use: Reels, carousels, static photos, occasional Stories
- Best performing post ever: A reel showing my no-waste kitchen routine (148K views on TikTok)
- Goal: Reach 50K followers on Instagram within 6 months and close first brand deal within 90 days

Please run a full account audit based on this information and deliver:
1. Profile Health Score (0–100) with breakdown by category
2. Content mix assessment — am I posting the right formats in the right ratio?
3. Top 3 things that are holding my growth back right now
4. 30/60/90-day growth roadmap with specific milestones
5. Week 1 sprint — the first 7 actions I should take starting Monday
```

## Key Patterns

1. **Audit before action**: The Growth Coach audit shapes everything downstream — don't skip it or rush it
2. **Data informs creativity**: The Analyst findings tell the Viral Architect exactly which formulas to prioritize for this specific account
3. **Community before monetization**: Audience Intelligence runs before Brand Deal Strategist because brand pitch quality depends on knowing the audience cold
4. **Paste full outputs**: Don't summarize — pass the complete output from each agent into the next step's prompt
5. **Weekly loop is non-negotiable**: One-time strategy without feedback loops stagnates — use the Analyst weekly to keep compounding

## Platform Coverage

- **TikTok**: Standard videos, trend content, series
